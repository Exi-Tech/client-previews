# Crowned By Bleu

Owner folder: Hayiqa. Variants: Shade and Ticket. Shared assets live in `assets/`.

## Why these templates

- **Shade** supports the business’s crown-focused name, blue-and-gold identity, private-suite experience and large verified portfolio with an elegant care-led story.
- **Ticket** is the contrasting appointment-first option because the official Square site publishes booking requirements, deposits and service categories.

## Sources checked 2026-09-10

- Supplied lead: phone, Suite 101 address, hours, Google rating snapshot (5.0 / 61), complete five-star breakdown, accessibility attributes, booking URL, Place ID and exact listing image. Carrier information and dummy email were excluded.
- Official Square site: https://crowned-by-bleu.square.site/. It confirms the Crowned By Bleu identity, Leah Bleu branding, portfolio images, Square booking and categories Kids (5–11), Adult (12+), Maintenance, Treatments, Add Ons and Protective Styles.
- Official Square artwork states a $25 deposit and virtual consultation requirement for first-time clients, with instructions communicated by text and email. The preview leaves the detailed policy on Square.
- Official socials published by the Square site: Facebook `kliyah.coleman.7`, Instagram `leahbleu`, and TikTok `@leahbleuu`.
- My Salon Suite independently identifies Leah Bleu / Crowned By Bleu as a private-suite loc and hair professional. Its cached listing says Suite 206 and a different phone, conflicting with the supplied current Google record. The preview therefore uses the supplied Google address (Suite 101) and phone.
- No prices or testimonials were invented. Google figures are labelled as a supplied snapshot.
