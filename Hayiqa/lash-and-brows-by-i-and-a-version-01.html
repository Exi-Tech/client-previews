Meet the studio

Where beauty feels personal.

Lash & Brows by I & A is a cozy Downtown Tampa studio focused on detailed lash artistry and beautiful brow design. Founder Inna Safarova is a licensed lash artist with a decade of experience across classic, hybrid, volume and mega-volume sets, along with lash lifts and brow services.

The goal is simple: a comfortable appointment, thoughtful styling and a result that feels like the prettiest version of you.
