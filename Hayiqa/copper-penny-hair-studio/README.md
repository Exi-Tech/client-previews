# Copper Penny Hair Studio

Owner folder: Hayiqa. Variants: Shade and Pinboard. Shared assets live in `assets/`.

## Why these templates

- **Shade** fits a salon strongly associated with color, highlights, curly hair and makeovers, giving those strengths a polished consultation-led presentation.
- **Pinboard** provides a warmer neighborhood alternative centered on the salon’s distinctive copper interior and frequently praised atmosphere.

## Sources checked 2026-09-10

- Supplied lead: phone, address, hours, Google rating snapshot (4.7 / 171), rating breakdown, women-owned and accessibility attributes, Place ID, exact listing image and Facebook URL. Unrelated personal and carrier data were excluded.
- Verified Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/thecopperpennyhairstudiolkld. The page identity and profile image match the exact Lakeland salon.
- Independent current listings consistently confirm the address, phone, hours, haircuts, coloring and hairstyling. Recent public feedback also consistently mentions highlights, curly-hair work, perms, blowouts and the studio atmosphere.
- No verified Instagram or online booking provider was found, so the previews prioritize calls, Facebook and directions.
- No testimonials, staff biographies or prices were copied into the pages. Google rating figures are identified as the supplied snapshot.
